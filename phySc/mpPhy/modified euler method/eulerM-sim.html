The predictor-corrector method is also known as Modified-Euler method. In the Euler method, the tangent is drawn at a point and slope is calculated for a given step size. Thus this method works best with linear functions, but for other cases, there remains a truncation error. To solve this problem the Modified Euler method is introduced. In this method instead of a point, the arithmetic average of the slope over an interval is used.
